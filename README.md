# Happy Birthday Ankan! 🕷️🎂

A Spider-Man themed interactive 15th birthday surprise website for **Ankan**!

## ✨ Features

- 🕷️ **Spider-Man Comic Aesthetic**: Halftone dots, vibrant Marvel color scheme, dynamic comic burst elements, and responsive typography.
- 🎵 **Sound & Music**:
  - Background music (`music.mp3`) with play/pause dock control.
  - **Synthesized Web Audio SFX Engine**: Dynamic retro sound effects for web-slinging (*THWIP!*), combat hits, candle flames, candle blowing, wish locking, and victory fanfare.
- 🕸️ **Interactive Web-Shooting**:
  - Click or tap anywhere on the background or spiders to shoot interactive spiderwebs radiating across the screen with comic action tags (*THWIP!*, *POW!*, *ZAP!*, *WHAM!*).
- 🕷️ **Roaming Spiders**:
  - Roaming animated spiders crawling across the screen with leg animations. Tap a spider to make it flip with a web burst and comic sound effects.
- 📸 **Two Sides of the Hero & Dossier Lightbox**:
  - Interactive gallery showcasing *Ankan The Original* and *Spider-Ankan The Hero*.
  - Tap any card to open the **Hero Dossier Lightbox** featuring secret superhero stats, background lore, and persona switching.
- 🎮 **Mini-Game ("Thwip the Villains!")**:
  - Whack-a-mole style superhero game with classic villains (Green Goblin 🎃, Doc Ock 🐙, Electro ⚡, Venom 🖤, Golden Spider 🕷️, and Cosmic Star ⭐).
  - Dynamic combo multiplier (`COMBO x2!`, `SPIDEY SENSE x3!`), floating score popups, and high score tracking with `localStorage`.
- 🎂 **Interactive Spider-Man Birthday Cake**:
  - Light 5 glowing birthday candles with animated flickering flames.
  - Blow them out with smoke physics.
  - **Secret 15th Birthday Wish Vault**: Type and seal a secret 15th birthday wish securely into the hero vault.
  - Cut the cake with an animated knife and slice movement.
- ✉️ **Emotional Brother's Letter**:
  - Unfolding birthday letter with life advice and love from Ankan's brother.
  - Extra confetti celebration button and sharing action.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive**:
  - Optimized for Smartphones, Tablets, and PCs with touch controls and safe area insets.

## 🚀 Local Development

```bash
node serve.js
```
Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.

## 🌐 Deploy to Vercel

This site is pre-configured for instant zero-configuration deployment on [Vercel](https://vercel.com):
- `vercel.json` includes security and caching headers for high performance.
- Works out of the box as a static web application.
